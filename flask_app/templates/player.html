Team advantage versus on-court quality: the first is the gap between the sides, the second is the level both are playing at. Opponents +5 against teammates +4, and opponents −4 against teammates −5, are both a −1 gap but completely different games. Across the whole dataset the two measures correlate at only about −0.1, so they really are separate questions.

Ranks use each player's rating, and genuine ties share the better rank — two players tied at the top are both 1st and nobody is 2nd. Ties are about 4% of team-games and come almost entirely from players who share a tier rating.

Reading these: Win% is what happened, Win Prob is what the model expected going in. Gospel is the gap — positive means outperforming expectation in that role. A player who wins a lot as the 1st option but posts a negative Gospel there is winning on the strength of the teams they were given, not their own.
